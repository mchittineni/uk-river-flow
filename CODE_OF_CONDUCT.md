# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a straightforward, welcoming place to contribute,
regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people. Critique the change, not the author.
- Assume competence and good faith. Ask what someone was solving for before
  concluding they got it wrong.
- Accept that "no" is a legitimate answer to a proposal, and that maintainers owe
  you a reason but not agreement.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, or
publishing others' private information.

## Reporting

Open a confidential report by emailing the maintainer listed on the GitHub profile,
or use GitHub's "Report abuse" if you would rather not contact us directly.

Reports are read by the maintainers, kept confidential, and answered. Outcomes range
from a private word to a permanent ban, in proportion to what happened.

## Scope

Applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any project space
elsewhere where you are representing the project.
